## Overview & How to Load Luas

Luas are community scripts that expand Cherax's functionality with custom features like money methods, vehicle modding, trolling options, QOL improvements, and unique minigames.

### How to Install and Run Luas
1. Navigate to the **Cherax Loader** or the [Cherax Website](https://cherax.vip).
2. Find and **subscribe** to the Lua scripts you want.
3. In-game, open Cherax and navigate to **Settings**.
4. In the bottom right, click **Refresh Scripts** and load your desired script.

## Lua Directory by Category

### Money & Business

- **Silent Night** – Packs a wide range of useful features into Heist, Business, Money, and Dev tools (stat editing).
- **Codax** – Features an extensive Business Manager alongside a range of quality-of-life tools designed to reduce grind and speed up progression.
- **HustleMeter** – Tracks your GTA Online earnings over time with a HUD to help monitor earning limits.
- **Kortz Heist Helper** – Streamlines the Kortz Heist with automated setup, target selection, prep skipping, finale assistance, and award management.
- **Ji9sw - Gun Van** – Lets you modify the contents of the Gun Van and change its available weapon selection.

### Gameplay, Trolling & Fun

- **K-Script** – Powerful crashing, trolling, and quality-of-life features, including FiveM-style emotes, NAT/region spoofing, and advanced protections.
- **DaiveKit** – Fun self and vehicle features, trolling options, protections, money options, net spoofing, and more.
- **Chairhax** – Legendary script packed with an extensive CLI, custom structure creator with Menyoo support, attackers, trolling, and fun tools.
- **Impulse Lua** – Recreation of the classic Impulse menu adapted to work inside Cherax.
- **Trolley** – Troll-oriented script with airstrikes and fun features.
- **VibeScript** – 60+ features including vehicle modifications, session tools, trolling options, drift assist, rewind, and various quality-of-life improvements.
- **!Chat Commands** – Adds chat commands for healing, repairs, upgrades, vehicles, bodyguards, teleportation, weather, and more.
- **Fireworks Menu** – Brings back the classic GTA Online firework menu from original-gen consoles.
- **CheraxDOOM** – Play DOOM inside Cherax with support for custom Doom 1 and Doom 2 WAD files.
- **Minigames** – Turns GTA Online sessions into an arcade with WarGames, The Purge, Land On It, DriftGod, and Beast.

### Vehicles & Driving

- **BunnyScript** – Vehicle customization and spawning with support for Cherax, Stand, and Lexis vehicle files, live previews, tattoo stacking, and Stand outfits.
- **BiggerScript** – Spawn thousands of modded vehicles, maps, and outfits, featuring a built-in spooner to create your own.
- **VROOM** – Adds manual-transmission features alongside custom vehicle handling, homing horn rockets, horn boosts, and other vehicle options.
- **DriftGod** – Arcade-style drifting system with momentum slides, combo chaining, stunt tiers, and progression.
- **Crimson Vehicles** – Adds various modded vehicles and allows you to spawn different vehicles.
- **Flying Cars** – Brings the classic San Andreas flying cars cheat into Cherax with flight controls, stunt tracking, and landing mechanics.
- **Ji9sw - Racing Lines** – Shows your driving path with speed-based colors and lets you teleport back along your route.
- **Aerials** – Dedicated suite for aircraft enthusiasts, including flight tools, HUD, and custom weapons.

### PvP & Combat

- **PvP Helper** – PvP-focused tools including aircraft aimbot, target locking, 3D FOV, Smooth Aim, and Ghost Aim.
- **Electrical Orbits** – Adds magical electric orbs that orbit around your player and can be used for attacks.
- **elloscript** – Adds player, NPC, and self ESP, PvP tools, vehicle controls, trolling options, recovery utilities, and quality-of-life features.

### Session & Host Tools

- **Host Tools** – Session-hosting tools for detecting, kicking, and managing modders, alongside RID blocking, NAT spoofing, session logs, and a host HUD.
- **Startup** – Automates GTA Online session startup, lobby advertising, player joining, NAT spoofing, region advertising, and other session-hosting functions.
- **Cherax Chat** – Work-in-progress global chat with a Discord bridge.
- **Discord RPC Reborn** – Displays your active GTA session and activity through Discord.

### Utilities & Quality of Life

- **Spooner** – Adds a Spooner-style object and map placement mode similar to Menyoo.
- **Extended Constructor** – Allows you to spawn, attach, manipulate, save, and load objects and peds.
- **2take1 Lua** – Recreates the 2Take1 menu within Cherax.
- **CyberPilot HUD** – Adds an enhanced HUD with a compass, satellite map, player list, and modder display.
- **Bind Manager** – Expands Cherax's keybind system with controller, mouse, and side-button support.

### Translators

- **Auto Translator** – Detects your GTA language and automatically loads the appropriate translation.
- **CheemsTranslator** – Real-time GTA Online chat translation, multiple language support, chat reactions, and session tools.

### Bodyguards

- **BetterBodyguards** – A comprehensive and polished bodyguard script with extended bodyguard capabilities.
- **OptimizedBodyguards** – Lets you spawn helicopter and vehicle escorts to protect you or attack others.

### Outfits

- **OutfitModeler** – Dedicated outfit editor with model previews, a resizable GUI, and easier outfit customization.
- **OutfitManager** – Imports `.json` outfits into Cherax in-game outfit slots.
- **LiquidFit - Outfit Manager** – In-depth outfit editor with previews for 27,000+ clothing items, searchable item names, outfit saving, and sharing.
- **Outfit Editor** – Allows you to rename and manage your saved outfits.
- **MeowPartyEmotes** – Adds accessory animations, custom backpacks, tattoo stacking, vehicle attachment features, and custom outfits.
