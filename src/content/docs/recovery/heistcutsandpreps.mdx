> **Important note:** ALWAYS stay in the money earning limits, as for heists those values are **30 Mil** a day and **150 Mil** a week

## How much can heists pay?

Depends on which heist, here are the major ones and their payouts, sorted by highest first:
- Apartment Heist (15 mil if you include the 12 mil bonus, 3 mil if not)
- Diamond Casino (3.6 mil)
- Cayo Perico (2.55 mil)
- Doomsday (2.55 mil)

## How much can I earn and how often?

If you do the 12 mil bonus you can rake up to 20 mil in an hour, if not (just the regular apartment heist 3 mil), you can rake in about 11 mil per 20-30 min.

## How much can my friends earn alongside me?

Depends on the amount of friends as for example Fleeca Job is a 2 player only job, and you also have Pacific Standard which is 4 players. In most cases you're looking at about 8 mil per 20-30 min with them.
